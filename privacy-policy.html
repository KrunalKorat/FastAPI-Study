Data Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

Clear, honest disclosures on how FastAPI Study protects your privacy, manages local storage, and guarantees zero persistence for your educational assessments.

Last Updated: October 2026 Scope: Static Educational Website Repository: KrunalKorat/FastAPI-Study

01. Overview & Educational Philosophy

FastAPI Study is an independent, non-commercial educational project created solely for studying FastAPI and modern Python REST API development. It is completely free to use for personal educational and practice purposes.

Educational & Non-Commercial Declaration: This website is not a commercial product, does not sell products, services, or subscriptions, and does not process payments or solicit donations. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by FastAPI or Sebastián Ramírez.

02. Absolute Zero-Persistence Policy for Quiz Attempts

The core privacy guarantee of FastAPI Study is that your quiz activity is strictly transient:

  • When you take a quiz on quiz.html, your selected answers, text responses, and calculated scores are held exclusively in your browser's temporary JavaScript memory.
  • Your quiz answers and scores are never saved to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or HTTP cookies.
  • No quiz data is ever transmitted across a network, saved to a database, or shared with analytics providers.
  • The moment you reload the webpage, close the browser tab, or navigate to another page, all your responses and scores are permanently and irreversibly cleared.
Why this matters: You can practice questions, make mistakes, and test yourself with complete privacy. No history is tracked, logged, or recorded anywhere.

03. Technologies & Browser Storage Disclosures

FastAPI Study operates without user accounts, registration forms, or backend databases. We do not track users across websites or sell personal data.

The actual technologies and browser storage used by this website are:

  • fastapi_cookie_consent (Local Storage): Stores your preference choice ("accepted" or "essential") so the consent dialog does not repeatedly re-appear on every page navigation.
  • fastapi_reader_font_size (Local Storage): Retains your chosen textbook reader typography size (small, regular, or large) for comfortable reading sessions.
  • Zero Quiz Persistence (Session Memory Only): Your quiz answers, attempts, and calculated scores live strictly in transient JavaScript browser memory and are permanently discarded when you reload or navigate away.
  • YouTube No-Cookie Video Embeds: Video curriculum embeds use YouTube's official no-cookie domain (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube sets cookies only if and when you actively choose to click and play a video.
  • Static Hosting Logs: Standard web hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel and GitHub Pages) processes transient technical network logs (IP addresses, User-Agent, and timestamps) strictly for security, DDoS protection, and content delivery.

04. Hosting & Third-Party Infrastructure

FastAPI Study is deployed as a static website on modern cloud hosting (such as Vercel and GitHub Pages). Like virtually all web servers, the hosting infrastructure may process standard HTTP network request logs (including IP addresses, browser User-Agent strings, and request timestamps) to maintain network security, mitigate DDoS attacks, and deliver static assets efficiently. These server-level logs are governed by the respective hosting provider's privacy policy.

Typography assets are loaded via Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Poppins, JetBrains Mono). Google Fonts serves font stylesheets and files without requiring authentication cookies.

05. External Links & GitHub Integration

Our website includes links to our public open-source repository at https://github.com/KrunalKorat/FastAPI-Study. When navigating to GitHub, your interaction is subject to GitHub's privacy statement and terms of service.

06. Direct Email Contact

If you choose to contact the creator via email at krunalkorat2004@gmail.com, your email address, message body, and metadata will be received in the author's email inbox solely for the purpose of reviewing technical feedback, answering inquiries, and correcting textbook errata. Your email will never be sold, leased, or added to automated marketing lists.

07. Your Rights & Control Over Stored Data

Because we do not maintain accounts or server databases, you retain complete physical control over any data on your computer:

  • You can reset or change your cookie preferences at any time via the Cookie Preferences page.
  • You can clear all site storage instantly by clearing your browser's cache or using your browser developer tools.
  • You can browse the website in Private / Incognito browsing mode without any persistent client storage.

08. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in educational content or hosting infrastructure. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
