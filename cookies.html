01. What Are Cookies and Browser Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. Similar technologies include HTML5 Local Storage (localStorage), Session Storage (sessionStorage), and client-side memory. These technologies allow websites to remember user preferences, maintain session state, and operate cleanly.

02. What Technologies Does FastAPI Study Actually Use?

FastAPI Study is a pure, static educational reference. We do not use third-party tracking scripts, advertising networks, marketing pixels, cross-site trackers, or commercial analytics.

The only client storage technologies utilized on this website are two minimal, privacy-respecting local storage keys:

Key Name Type Classification Purpose Retention
fastapi_study_theme localStorage Essential Preference Remembers your visual comfort choice (Warm Ivory vs Dark reading mode). Persistent until cleared
fastapi_cookie_consent localStorage Essential Consent Remembers whether you clicked "Accept" or "Essential only" so the banner does not repeatedly bother you. Persistent until cleared

03. Zero Persistence for Quiz Data

Under no circumstances are your quiz answers, question choices, test scores, or completion percentages stored in cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or transmitted across a network.

In-Memory Guarantee: Your quiz attempt lives solely in transient JavaScript browser memory during the active session. When you refresh the webpage or navigate away, every question response and computed score is permanently discarded.

04. Essential vs. Optional Technologies

Because this website does not deploy third-party advertising or analytics cookies, choosing "Essential only" retains 100% of website functionality, including the digital book reader, search index, code copying, and chapter quizzes. Choosing "Accept optional cookies" grants permission to retain UI preferences.

05. How to Clear Browser Storage Manually

You can wipe stored preferences at any time using your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.

You can also click the "Reset Consent Choice" button in the interactive card above to clear your stored choice on this device immediately.
