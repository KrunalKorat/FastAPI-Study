Test Your Mastery with Zero Data Footprint

FastAPI Study is engineered as an honest, private educational reference. We believe technical study should never require logins, tracking cookies, or surveillance.

  • In-Memory Evaluation Only

    Answers are scored instantly in browser memory against authoritative answer keys. No answers are ever sent across a network.

  • No Local Storage or Cookies for Quizzes

    We do not store test scores in localStorage, IndexedDB, or cookies. When you reload or navigate away, the attempt disappears.

  • Authoritative Explanations & Model Solutions

    Every multiple choice question and code debugging problem includes the original detailed line-by-line architectural breakdown.
